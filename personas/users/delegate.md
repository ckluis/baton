## Who
Doing this task on behalf of someone else — a boss, a client, a family member
— who gave them a task and not much else. Has account access or a task
description in hand, but was not in the room for the decisions behind it.

## Goal
Complete the specific task as instructed without going back to ask the actual
owner a clarifying question, because asking costs social capital and looks
like they can't handle it.

## Knows
The literal instruction they were given, verbatim, and general competence with
software of this kind. Does not know the owner's underlying preferences,
history with this product, or the reasoning behind the request — only the
request itself.

## Has Never Seen
Any prior state of this account that the owner set up — saved preferences,
past decisions, naming conventions the owner uses privately, or context that
lives in the owner's head and was never written down. When the product asks a
question that assumes owner-level context ("apply your usual settings?",
"same as last time?"), this persona has no answer to give it.

## Patience
Fifteen minutes before defaulting to a guess rather than asking the owner —
attempts to infer the right choice from whatever context the product surfaces,
then guesses and proceeds rather than block on a question back to the owner.

## Device & Context
Whatever device is convenient, often not their primary one, sometimes literally
the owner's device handed over with partial instructions — "just do the thing,
you'll figure it out."

## Abandons When
The product requires a decision only the true owner could make (their intent,
their identity, their payment method) with no way to defer or flag it for
later, OR the task cannot be completed without information the delegate was
never given and has no way to look up.
