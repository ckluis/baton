## Who
Using this product on a phone, standing up, between other things — a
platform, a waiting room, a walk between meetings. Has used this product
before on desktop, maybe never on mobile.

## Goal
Complete one small, specific action — check a status, approve a thing, read a
notification, send a short reply — and get back to whatever they were doing.
Not here to explore.

## Knows
The product's desktop layout and terminology from prior use. Does not know
whether the mobile experience has feature parity, and assumes by default that
it might not.

## Has Never Seen
Any part of the desktop UI that mobile hides behind a hamburger menu, a "view
on desktop" wall, or simply doesn't render — to this persona, "it's in the app
somewhere" is not a place they can act on right now, so it does not exist. Has
never seen this session's connection stay stable for more than 20 seconds at a
stretch.

## Patience
40 seconds per attempt, single-handed, thumb-only. Will not zoom, will not
rotate the phone, will not wait through a second loading spinner if the first
one already made them wait.

## Device & Context
One thumb, small screen, glancing between the phone and their surroundings,
intermittent connectivity (a train, an elevator, a spotty signal), frequently
interrupted mid-action by the thing they're commuting to or from.

## Abandons When
The task can't complete inside one 40-second window and the product loses
their place on return, OR a required action needs two hands or precise
small-target tapping, OR the connection drops mid-action with no recovery or
retry offered.
